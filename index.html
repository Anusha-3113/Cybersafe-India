Learn through animated mini-stories

Short visual lessons: spot the warning sign, pause, and choose a safer next step.

📱💸⚠️

The urgent UPI request

Someone says “pay now” or asks you to scan a QR code to receive money.

Pause. You do not enter a UPI PIN to receive money. Verify independently with the person or business using a trusted number.
📩🔗🛡️

The suspicious link

A message claims your account will be blocked unless you sign in immediately.

Do not tap the link. Open the official app or type the known website address yourself. Never share an OTP or password.
📞😰🧠

The frightening phone call

A caller creates panic with threats about a family member or police case.

Pause and verify through another trusted contact. Do not send money because of a threatening call. If there is immediate danger, call 112.

More situations to learn about

💼

Fake job offer

Someone asks for a registration fee, training fee, or sensitive documents before a job.

👮

Digital arrest threat

A caller claims you are under investigation and demands money or that you stay on a video call.

🔢

OTP or account takeover

Someone asks for a one-time code or tricks you into approving a login.

☎️

Fake customer care

A search result or social post gives a fake support number or asks for remote access.

📈

Investment scam

A group promises guaranteed profits or asks for more money to release your balance.

🔒

Ransomware or malware

A device behaves strangely, files are locked, or a suspicious app was installed.

These are educational examples, not a detector. Scams can look different; verify through official channels.

Before you report

  • Save screenshots, messages, URLs, phone numbers, emails, dates, and payment references.
  • Do not pay someone who promises to recover stolen money for a fee.
  • Never share OTPs, passwords, UPI PINs, or remote-access codes.
  • Use only official links. Check the web address before entering information.
  • For immediate danger, contact emergency services rather than waiting for an online form.
Privacy: This prototype does not have an account system or send your complaint draft to a server. Avoid using a shared device for sensitive notes, and close the page when finished. Official government portals may request identity or mobile verification.
